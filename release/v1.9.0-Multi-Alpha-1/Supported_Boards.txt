FPVGate v1.9.0-Multi-Alpha-1 - Supported Boards

  - ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 (8MB Flash)
  - FPVGate AIO V3 (XIAO ESP32S3, 8MB)
  - FPVGate Solo (XIAO ESP32S3, 8MB)
  - Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 (8MB)
  - XIAO ESP32S3 Plus (16MB Flash)

USB networking (http://192.168.7.1 over USB-C) is available on all of
these. Windows and Linux only; macOS does not support RNDIS.
